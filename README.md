# SoftwareProject-Team04

This is the airport kiosk website for our software engineering presentation. It’s a demo, so the flights, bookings and passport scans are examples you can actually click through.

To try it, [download the ZIP](https://github.com/ahmedlikescar/SoftwareProject-Team04/raw/refs/heads/main/airport-kiosk-prototype.zip), unzip it, and open `index.html`. That’s it—no setup or internet needed. You can also [look at the website file](airport-kiosk-prototype/index.html) here on GitHub.

## What to show in the presentation

1. **Start on Home.** Switch between English and Arabic using the button at the top.
2. **Check a flight.** Search `EK203` to see a boarding flight, or `FZ442` to see a delay. The flight screen also has a sample arrivals and departures board.
3. **Pull up a booking.** Go to **My booking** and enter `DXB482` with `FERAS` for Tawfiq Feras. `UAE729` with `SHERIF` brings up Ahmed Sherif. You can also try the demo passport scan.
4. **Print the boarding pass.** Open either booking and press **Print boarding pass**. Your browser will let you choose a printer or save it as a PDF.
5. **Try Smart Gate.** The sample passports show approval, rejection, and what happens after three unreadable scans.
6. **Find your way around.** Pick a gate or facility on **Airport map**, then open **Discover UAE** for the visitor info and QR summaries.
7. **Ask Travel help.** Try a question about a flight, gate, baggage or transport. The answers work offline.
8. **Show the staff side.** Open **Equipment and privacy** to see the passport memory clear after 10 seconds. The **Staff / IT Admin** demo login is `admin` / `swe401` and includes the FAQ editor and status screen.

It’s a presentation demo, so it doesn’t connect to real airport systems. The [full guide](airport-kiosk-prototype/README.md) has a few more details.
